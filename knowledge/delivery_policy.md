# Delivery policy

## Delivery time
- Orders approved before 2 pm are packed the same working day.
- Delivery takes 2 to 5 working days, depending on the city.
- There is no delivery on Sundays and public holidays.

## Delivery charges
- Orders of 10,000 rupees or more are delivered free.
- For smaller orders, a delivery charge of 150 rupees is added to the invoice.

## When goods are not in stock
- If a product is out of stock, the customer is told the same day.
- The customer can wait for the full order or take a partial delivery. Partial deliveries are invoiced for the goods sent.
- Goods on a supplier reorder usually arrive within 7 days of the reorder being approved.

## Delivery problems
- If a delivery is late by more than 3 days, the customer should email us with the order number. We contact the transport company and reply within 1 working day.
