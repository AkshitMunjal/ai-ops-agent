# Payment terms

## Standard terms
- Every invoice is payable within 30 days of the invoice date.
- An invoice that is not paid after 30 days is called overdue.
- Payment is accepted by bank transfer and by UPI. Cheques are not accepted.

## Reminders
- When an invoice is overdue, the customer gets a payment reminder by email.
- The same invoice is reminded at most once every 7 days.
- Every reminder is read and sent by a person. Nothing is sent automatically.

## Late payment
- A customer with an invoice overdue by more than 60 days gets no new order approved until the invoice is paid.
- A customer with a payment problem can ask for a written payment plan. The owner decides on each plan.

## Marking an invoice as paid
- An invoice is marked as paid only after the money has arrived in the bank account.
- A paid invoice never gets a reminder.
