# Stock and reorder policy

## Reorder level
- Every product has a reorder level. When the stock falls to this level or below, the product needs a reorder.
- The reorder quantity is enough to bring the stock to twice the reorder level.

## How a reorder works
1. Every morning at 08:00 the system checks the stock and prepares one draft reorder for each supplier.
2. The owner or the stock manager reads the draft and approves it, or cancels it.
3. After approval, the order is sent to the supplier.
4. When the goods arrive, a person marks the reorder as received. Only then the stock numbers go up.

## Rules
- A product that is already on a draft or approved reorder is not ordered again until the goods are received.
- Only people approve reorders. The automatic system only prepares drafts.
- Reorders from one supplier are grouped into one order, to save on delivery.

## Checking incoming goods
- When goods arrive, the quantity and the condition are checked against the reorder before it is marked as received.
- Any damaged or missing goods are reported to the supplier on the same day.
