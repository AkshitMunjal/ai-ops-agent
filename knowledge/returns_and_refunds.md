# Returns and refunds

## What can be returned
- Goods that arrive damaged or different from the order can be returned.
- Goods must be reported within 7 days of delivery.
- Goods that were ordered correctly and arrived in good condition can be returned only if they are unopened and in the original packing. These returns need the owner's approval.

## How to return
- The customer sends an email with the order number, the product and a photo of the problem.
- We reply within 2 working days and say if the return is accepted.
- Return shipping for damaged or wrong goods is paid by us. Return shipping for any other return is paid by the customer.

## Refunds
- A refund is made as a credit note or as a bank transfer, as the customer prefers.
- A refund is made within 7 working days after the returned goods are received and checked.
- Custom-made or special-order items cannot be refunded.
