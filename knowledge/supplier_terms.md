# Supplier terms

## Paying suppliers
- Supplier invoices are paid within 30 days of receiving the goods, unless the supplier agreed another term in writing.
- Suppliers are paid by bank transfer only.

## Ordering from suppliers
- Reorders are sent to the supplier by email after a person approves them.
- A supplier is asked to confirm each order within 2 working days.
- If a supplier cannot deliver on time, the stock manager is told and decides whether to wait or to order from another supplier.

## Damaged or wrong goods from a supplier
- The supplier is told on the day the goods are checked.
- Damaged or wrong goods are returned at the supplier's cost, or replaced.
- Goods that are returned are not counted in the stock.

## Prices
- Purchase prices are agreed with each supplier and stay fixed for the period written in the agreement.
- A price change is accepted only after the owner has approved it in writing.
